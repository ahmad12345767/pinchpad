Questions

What do I need to run PinchPad?

A Mac with macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. The pinch gesture needs a trackpad; without one, use the keyboard shortcut or the menu bar icon.

How do I install it?
  1. After buying, download the zip file and open it.
  2. Drag PinchPad into your Applications folder and open it.
  3. If macOS says it can't verify the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.
Will it fight with macOS's own pinch gesture?

No. macOS 26 removed the switch for its pinch gesture, so PinchPad has a one-click Turn Off macOS Pinch button in its settings. You can turn it back on just as easily.

Does PinchPad need any permissions?

None. PinchPad works right away, with no special permissions to grant.

Does it collect any data?

No. PinchPad has no accounts, no analytics and never connects to the internet. Everything stays on your Mac.

Is PinchPad made by Apple?

No. PinchPad is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
